Retro Sudoku privacy policy

Developer and contact

Retro Sudoku is developed by Varshbyte Lab. Privacy contact: varshbytelab@gmail.com. Effective date: November 3, 2026.

Game data on your device

Puzzles, saved games, settings, statistics, lesson completion, achievements and daily streaks are stored on your device. The app does not require its own account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data; Android device backup and restore may retain or restore eligible app data according to your system settings.

Notifications

The app schedules a local Sudoku reminder about every four hours when Android notification permission and the app's notification channel allow it. Android battery restrictions can delay delivery. On Android 13 or newer, the app requests notification permission once. There is no separate in-app subscription or reminder time setting. Disable reminders through Android's notification settings. Reminder scheduling, delivery times and local streak data stay on your device.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob for interstitial and rewarded advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics under their policies. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required and checks whether ads may be requested before loading or displaying them. Where required, reopen Ad privacy choices in Settings. A free hint does not require an ad; additional hints require choosing to watch a rewarded ad. An optional completed rewarded ad unlocks one selected display mode (AMOLED, Midnight or Sepia) for 24 hours. Each mode requires its own ad and has its own expiry. Occasional interstitial ads may appear when leaving Statistics. Consent availability depends on region and the messages configured by the publisher.

Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy Google advertising information: Google advertising information

Usage analytics and crash reports

The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or a developer-operated analytics server. Local statistics and achievements stay on the device. Advertising and Google Play Games services may still process data under their own policies as described here.

Google Play Games

When the publisher has configured Google Play Games Services, the app can authenticate with your Google Play Games profile and submit unlocked achievements. Google handles the profile, authentication and online achievement records under its policies. Offline progress remains local; your local puzzle save is not a cloud-save feature. Google Play Games is unavailable in builds without its configuration.

Data security

Local game data is stored in the app’s storage area, protected by Android’s application access controls. Device backups follow your Android backup settings. Keep your device and operating system secure. Advertising and Google Play Games data handling is governed by the providers’ policies linked here.

Sharing, retention and choices

The app has no developer-operated account server. Service providers process data as described above and retain it according to their policies and configured retention settings. You can clear local app data, disable Android notifications, change ad privacy choices when offered, and manage your Play Games data through Google settings. Contact the publisher about privacy requests; applicable rights depend on your jurisdiction and the service processing the data.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Contact varshbytelab@gmail.com with privacy questions.
